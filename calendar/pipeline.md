## 🎯 Cross-Posting & Funnel Rhythm

1. **Day 1 (Anchor Launch)**:
   - Publish long-form essay on **Medium**.
   - Publish teaser thread on **BlueSky** linking to Medium.
2. **Day 2 (Engagement & Short Video)**:
   - Release 60s **YouTube Short / TikTok** visual hook with link in bio / pinned comment.
   - Reply to comments on BlueSky and Medium to seed discussions.
3. **Day 4 (Follow-up Discussion / Quote Card)**:
   - Share a key quote or diagram from the essay on BlueSky with the interactive discussion prompt.
