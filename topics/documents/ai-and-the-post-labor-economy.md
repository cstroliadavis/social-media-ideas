## 1. Deep-Dive Knowledge & Context

### Core Insight

When cognitive labor reaches near-zero marginal cost, the foundational premise of exchanging human
labor for survival collapses, requiring new economic models.

### Counter-Perspective (Steel-Manning)

Technological revolutions historically destroy old jobs while creating new, higher-level creative
industries.

### Nuances & Blind Spots

- Practical implementation edge cases when adopting this approach in production environments.
- Distinguishing between dogmatic application and context-sensitive pragmatism.
